FocusPals

A friendly focus coach for your phone. Set limits on the apps that pull you in, keep a streak going, and stay in charge of your time.

How FocusPals helps

Simple tools, a little encouragement, and data that stays on your phone.

  • 📱

    Pick your distractions

    Choose the apps you want to manage and set a daily open limit and a time limit that feel right for you.

  • 🔥

    Build a streak

    When you stay within your limits, your streak grows. A gentle prompt helps you pause before a habit takes over.

  • 🪙

    Earn coins

    Stick with your goals and earn coins you can use for upgrades and rewards as FocusPals grows.
