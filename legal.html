01. PRIVACY POLICY

The short version

Glooba apps do not require an account. We do not sell your data or use advertising trackers. The information FocusPals needs to work such as the apps you choose to manage, your limits, daily open counts, streak and coins is stored on your device.

To improve FocusPals, we send anonymous product-analytics events to PostHog, our analytics processor. Those events do not include the names or package identifiers of the apps you use, and they do not include your name, email address or other personal details. See Analytics below.

This policy applies to Glooba’s apps, including FocusPals (Android package dev.glooba.focuspals), and to glooba.dev. If we introduce a feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this page before that feature is released.

02. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Data used by the app

To provide focus prompts and progress features, FocusPals stores the following information locally on your device:

  • the apps you choose to manage, including their displayed names and package identifiers;
  • your chosen limits for those apps;
  • daily open counts for the apps you choose to manage; and
  • app preferences and progress information, such as your streak and in-app coins.

This information is used only to run the app’s focus features. It is not uploaded to Glooba or to PostHog, shared with advertisers, or used to identify you. The current version does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, messages, photos, payment details, or the contents of other apps.

Our website does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

03. ANALYTICS

How we use PostHog

FocusPals uses PostHog, a product-analytics service provided by PostHog Inc. Analytics data is sent to PostHog’s European Union cloud (eu.i.posthog.com) and stored in the EU.

What PostHog receives:

  • anonymous usage events, such as onboarding steps (for example, starting or finishing onboarding) and whether the accessibility permission was granted, declined, or the system settings screen was opened;
  • anonymous counts related to the focus prompt, such as how often the prompt is shown and whether you continue or go back, together with numeric limits (for example, your chosen daily open limit and time limit, and how many times a tracked app was opened that day). We do not send the name or package identifier of any app; and
  • automatic technical events and properties that the PostHog software may attach, such as that the app was opened or backgrounded, the app version, device type, operating system version, and an anonymous identifier stored on your device. We do not use this to identify you as a person.

We do not use session replay, and we do not turn on interface autocapture. We do not ask PostHog to create a named person profile for you.

We use this information to understand how FocusPals is used and to improve the app. Our legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in improving FocusPals (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

PostHog Inc. acts as our processor. We keep analytics data only for as long as reasonably necessary to improve FocusPals, and we review this periodically.

There is currently no in-app toggle to opt out of analytics. You can uninstall FocusPals at any time, which stops further events from being sent. You can also contact us at info@glooba.dev with questions or requests about analytics data we process.

04. ACCESSIBILITY PERMISSION

Why FocusPals asks for access

On Android, FocusPals may ask you to enable the Accessibility Service. This lets the app recognise when an app you selected is in the foreground and display its focus prompt. It does not read the content of your screen, messages, typing, passwords, or other app windows. The name of the foreground app is used only on your device to show the prompt; it is not uploaded.

You are always in control. You can disable the Accessibility Service in your Android settings. When it is disabled, the focus prompt feature will no longer work.

05. YOUR CHOICES

Control and deletion

You can stop managing an app by removing it from FocusPals. You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time in Android settings. To remove locally stored app data, use your device’s app-storage controls or uninstall FocusPals. Because the focus data described above stays on your device, Glooba cannot access, correct, export, or restore it for you.

If you contact us by email, we use the information in your message only to reply and provide support. We keep support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain our records.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have data-protection rights, including rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and complaint to your local data-protection authority. Contact us using the details below and we will help where the request relates to data we process.
