01 — PRIVACY POLICY

The short version

Glooba apps do not require an account. We do not sell your data, use advertising trackers, or send your in-app focus data to Glooba servers. The information needed for the app to work is stored on your device.

This policy applies to Glooba’s apps, including ScreenPals, and to glooba.dev. If we introduce a feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this page before that feature is released.

02 — WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Data used by the app

To provide focus prompts and progress features, ScreenPals stores the following information locally on your device:

  • the apps you choose to manage, including their displayed names and package identifiers;
  • your chosen limits for those apps;
  • daily open counts for the apps you choose to manage; and
  • app preferences and progress information, such as your streak and in-app coins.

This information is used only to run the app’s focus features. It is not uploaded to Glooba, shared with advertisers, or used to identify you. The current version does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, messages, photos, payment details, or the contents of other apps.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs in the app. Our website does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

03 — ACCESSIBILITY PERMISSION

Why ScreenPals asks for access

On Android, ScreenPals may ask you to enable the Accessibility Service. This lets the app recognise when an app you selected is opened and display its focus prompt. It does not read the content of your screen, messages, typing, passwords, or other app windows.

You are always in control. You can disable the Accessibility Service in your Android settings. When it is disabled, the focus prompt feature will no longer work.

04 — YOUR CHOICES

Control and deletion

You can stop managing an app by removing it from ScreenPals. You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time in Android settings. To remove locally stored app data, use your device’s app-storage controls or uninstall ScreenPals. Because the current version does not receive a copy of your in-app data, Glooba cannot access, correct, export, or restore it for you.

If you contact us by email, we use the information in your message only to reply and provide support. We keep support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain our records.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have data-protection rights, including rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and complaint to your local data-protection authority. Contact us using the details below and we will help where the request relates to data we process.
